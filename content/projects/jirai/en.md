VRCX-jirai is a modified branch of the popular VRChat assistant VRCX, specifically designed to enhance social data visualization and relationship tracking. It aims to provide deep insights into co-presence history and social dynamics within the VRChat community.

## Key Features

- **Two-Person Relationship Query:** Analyze every instance where two selected friends were in the same room.
- **Bio Diff View:** Git-style interface to track changes in user biographies over time.
- **Mutual Friend Graph Enhancement:** Better visualization and manual connection management for social circles.
- **Status Distribution Charts:** Visual breakdown of online time spent in various statuses (Green/Blue/Yellow/Red).
